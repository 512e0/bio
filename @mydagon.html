01 / about

black boxes
bother me.

i’ve been programming for over eight years, and most of that time has gone into the machine itself: exploring systems, writing software, and working out why technology behaves the way it does.

about 70 projects later, many of them quietly useful to people i’ve never met, my repositories have collected over 2,000 stars.

what pulls me in is the layer underneath. linux internals, low-level programming, disassembly, game hacking. i don’t like treating software as a black box. i want to read its internals, watch its behavior, and take its mechanisms apart until they make sense.
